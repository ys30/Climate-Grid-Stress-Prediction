# Ready-to-send source-inquiry templates (NOT SENT)

These messages request publicly releasable or confidentiality-compliant source
metadata, not private respondent records. User/researcher must approve and send
each message. Source requests do not themselves grant E2 model admission.

## Message A: CEC annual consumption data custodian

**To:** QFERDemand@energy.ca.gov
**Subject:** Research data inquiry: nonconfidential county-by-utility electricity consumption (CEC-1306A, 2020–2023)

Hello CEC Energy Assessments / Data Collection and Analysis team,

I am conducting a reproducible climate–electricity research study involving
California balancing-authority load and weather exposure. I have reviewed
the California Electricity Consumption Dashboard and its public annual
county and utility aggregate workbooks. The county workbook reports
YEAR/COUNTY_NUM/COUNTY_NAME/SECTOR/RNR/GWH, while the agency workbook
reports YEAR/PLANNING_AREA/AGENCY_NAME/AGENCY_TYPE/SECTOR/RNR/GWH.
Neither appears to publish the joint county-by-distribution-utility
values needed for a careful spatial exposure assessment.

Could you please advise whether the CEC has a public, downloadable
dataset or a procedure to request a *nonconfidential, disclosure-screened
aggregate* for years 2020–2023, with year, county, utility distribution
company or agency identifier, sector and delivered/consumed electricity
in GWh? A monthly series would be helpful if already available, but
annual aggregates are sufficient for the initial study.

I would especially appreciate the relevant data dictionary, methodology,
reporting/revision vintages, description of self-generation versus
retail deliveries, confidentiality limitations and citation/license
conditions. If the joint geographic values are not releasable, could
you point me to any official alternative for utility-specific
county electricity consumption shares or clarify that such a public
dataset is unavailable?

My objective is to avoid presenting county and agency marginal totals
as if they identified actual county-by-utility electricity use. I
do not request personally identifiable customer information or any
confidential individual filing.

Thank you for any guidance or referral to the appropriate data team.

Best regards,
Yu

## Message B: CEC control-area reporting staff

**To:** QFERGEN@energy.ca.gov
**Subject:** Historical CEC-1305A/B/C control-area membership and hourly-load data (2020–2025)

Hello CEC Energy Assessments / QFER Generation team,

I am researching historical balancing-authority electricity demand
and geographic weather exposure in California. The CEC's official
Forms & Instructions page explains that control-area operator
reports CEC-1305A/B/C contain monthly system peak demand and
hourly load, utility distribution companies in each control
area, and changes when those distribution companies join or
leave a control area.

Could you advise whether the original historical monthly
CEC-1305A/B/C reports, or a nonconfidential public extract of
control-area-to-distribution-utility membership and effective
entry/exit dates, are available for 2020–2025?

In particular, I am trying to establish a year- or month-specific
crosswalk between California ISO's electricity balancing-authority
reporting scope and the distribution utilities that served its
electricity load. Even just a maintained respondent identity table,
control-area definitions, dated membership change history, or
data dictionary and archive location would help prevent
overclaiming geographic coverage.

If hourly control-area series are publicly releasable, I would
also appreciate the time standard, hour-ending convention,
original data/revision version identifiers and documentation
for comparability with EIA-930. Please let me know if there are
confidentiality restrictions or a more appropriate CEC contact.

I am requesting public/releasable historical source information,
not respondent-only portal credentials or private customer records.

Thank you for your time and assistance.

Best regards,
Yu
