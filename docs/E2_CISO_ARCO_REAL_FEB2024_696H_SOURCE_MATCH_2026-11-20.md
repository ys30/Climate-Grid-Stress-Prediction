## Interpretation restrictions

This is **source-clock interoperability only**:

- EIA-930 demand is converted from UTC hour-ending to UTC interval start.
- ERA5 2m temperature is instantaneous at UTC validity time, **not shifted**.
- Aligning these clocks does not prove physical causality, same-hour
  predictive utility, or CISO-wide weather conditions.
- Spatial aggregation must use documented CISO geography and grid-cell weights
  before constructing a CISO BA-hour weather variable.
- No regression, forecast-skill score, causal attribution, or 2030/2040/2050
  SSP/PyPSA stress prediction is claimed at this stage.
- CISO EIA 2020–25 source records are separately verified. Overall canonical
  source audit remains **23/24 BA-years**, with AZPS 2023 excluded from canonical
  pass due to two negative original demand values (noncanonical sensitivity
  artifact separately retained).
- C-1 source and results are frozen, untouched.
